How GoRaise Works

1. Create Your Project

Tell your story, set a funding goal, and create rewards for backers. Our team will review your project to ensure it meets our guidelines.

2. Share & Promote

Share your campaign on social media, email, and with your network. Engage with backers and keep them updated on your progress.

3. Receive Funding

If you reach your goal by the deadline, you'll receive the funds (minus fees). Start creating and fulfill your rewards to backers.

Frequently Asked Questions

GoRaise charges a 5% platform fee on successfully funded projects. Payment processing fees (2-3%) are additional.

Funds are transferred within 5-7 business days after your campaign ends successfully.

With our flexible funding option, you keep whatever you raise. With fixed funding, you get nothing if you don't reach your goal.
